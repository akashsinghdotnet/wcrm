# Wcrm Lead Forms — Official WordPress Plugin

Connect your WordPress website to Wcrm. Visually build unlimited lead capture forms with custom dropdowns, options, and fields that automatically sync directly to your CRM dashboard.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **1-Click Account Connection:** Connect your Wcrm account using your **Company ID** — no passwords needed.
- **Multi-Form Visual Builder:** Create unlimited custom forms (e.g. *"Homepage Footer Form"*, *"Service Inquiry Form"*, *"Contact Us Page"*).
- **Custom Dropdowns & Options:** Add custom dropdown menus, radio buttons, checkboxes, and textareas. Any custom option selected by a visitor is automatically compiled into the lead's **Notes/Message** in Wcrm!
- **Zero Coding Needed:** Copy & paste simple shortcodes like `[wcrm_form id="1"]` anywhere on your site (Pages, Posts, Widgets, Elementor, Gutenberg).
- **AJAX Submission:** Smooth, modern frontend UX without reloading the page.

---

## 🚀 Installation & Setup

1. **Zip the Plugin Folder:**
   Zip the `wcrm` directory into `wcrm.zip`.
2. **Install in WordPress:**
   - Go to your WordPress Admin → **Plugins** → **Add New** → **Upload Plugin**.
   - Choose `wcrm.zip` and click **Install Now**.
   - Click **Activate**.
3. **Connect Your Company Account:**
   - In your WordPress sidebar, go to **Wcrm** → **Settings**.
   - Paste your **Company ID** (found in your Wcrm account profile).
   - Click **Test Connection** to verify.
4. **Create a Custom Form:**
   - Go to **Wcrm** → **Add New Form**.
   - Add standard fields (Name, Phone, Email) or custom dropdowns and options.
   - Click **Save Form**.
5. **Embed on Your Website:**
   - Copy the shortcode `[wcrm_form id="X"]` and paste it into any page or widget!
